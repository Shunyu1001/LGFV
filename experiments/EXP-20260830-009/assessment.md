# Assessment

## Decision

Keep.

The experiment met its audit objective and returned an adverse result for the
broad novelty language. Prior work already distinguishes formal adoption from
practice and outcomes, studies official LGFV exit and market transformation,
warns that fiscal functions can persist across organizational labels, and
provides validation frameworks for text-derived labels. The hypothesis was
only partly sustained: none of the 24 material sources combined a documented
formal LGFV event, post-event tracing of the financing or project function,
and the same four mutually exclusive institutional fates. That absence within
the audited set does not justify a universal first-in-literature claim.

## Claim decisions

- C-007 should retire its broad claim that the formal-compliance versus
  functional-change framework is new. It should instead claim a bounded,
  LGFV-specific implementation: traceable case packets that join the event to
  post-event function location and assign one of four fates.
- C-008 should no longer say that historically rooted capacity operates
  through the three named mechanisms. External work supports them as possible
  contemporary processes and also supplies competing explanations. The claim
  should remain associational until distinct process indicators, timing, and
  negative evidence are coded.

## Integrity checks

- All 12 predeclared queries were sent once to each of two databases.
- All 24 database requests succeeded and their raw responses were retained.
- The screen retained adverse and conflicting sources.
- Twenty-four material sources were opened in original publications,
  repositories, proceedings, or authoritative institutional sites.
- Metadata records not opened from the 191-work manual-review pool remain
  unverified candidates rather than being treated as irrelevant. Targeted
  searches also rescued known close sources missed by broad-query metadata.
- Inaccessible material was quarantined or listed as unresolved.
- No central manuscript, bibliography, or ledger was changed.
