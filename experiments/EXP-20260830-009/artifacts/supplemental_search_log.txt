EXCLUSIONS AND UNRESOLVED ITEMS

- Search snippets, secondary summaries, news rewrites, and citation-only pages:
  excluded because no original evidence could be inspected.
- Duplicates: the published record was retained and an accessible author or
  repository manuscript was used only to inspect the text.
- Edelman (1992), Legal Ambiguity and Symbolic Structures: quarantined. The
  publisher record was verified, but the located author upload returned access
  errors; no page-level claim from it was used.
- Wan, Fan, and Wang (2024), Local Government Financing Vehicle Restructuring,
  Implicit Government Guarantees and Financing Costs: unresolved. Metadata and
  abstract were verified, but no complete original text was accessible.
- Sng (2014), Size and Dynastic Decline: excluded from the material set because
  only a preliminary full version was accessible and Chen, Kung, and Ma is more
  directly discriminating for the historical proxy used here.
- Bai and Jia (2016): excluded from the material set because it concerns
  revolutionary mobilization rather than contemporary administrative
  continuity and is already represented in the repository.
- Lu and Sun (2013): excluded because Wingender and Chen-He-Liu more directly
  discriminate formal fiscal absorption from financing migration.
- RBA (2024), The ABCs of LGFVs: opened as an authoritative synthesis but not
  counted among the 24 material sources because it is less discriminating than
  the primary studies selected.
- Ziems et al. (2024): opened but excluded under the 24-source budget because
  it is a broad roadmap rather than a dedicated validation or correction
  design.
- Hopkins and King and a later Halterman-Keith protest-codebook preprint:
  excluded under the source budget because the selected peer-reviewed sources
  more directly address four-class validation and downstream error.
- No source was included or excluded because of citation count, statistical
  significance, coefficient sign, or agreement with the manuscript.
