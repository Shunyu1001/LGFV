# Assessment

## Result

All primary, secondary, and supplementary identifiers used by the 94 gold
cases resolve uniquely against the tracked inventories. Every cited document
has a page or download URL. Ninety-three cases have complete local copies of
all cited documents. The Changshu Investment case has two resolvable document
records and two recovery URLs but no local copies. Sixty-two cases match a
dedicated evidence memo by exact case identifier or company name; 32 do not.

## Interpretation

The result establishes reference integrity and a strong recovery path. It does
not establish equal memo depth, independent recoding, source authenticity, or
continued remote availability. The case-level audit therefore remains a
reproducibility diagnostic and must not be presented as a measurement-validity
statistic.

## Hard gates

- Gold labels changed: no.
- Source inventories changed: no.
- Every cited identifier resolved: pass.
- Case-level negative findings retained: pass.
- Remote availability treated as permanent: no.

## Status
