# Guiyang identity, geography, and scope decision

## Origin and legal identity

The frozen display name is not a supported registered legal name. The origin
inventory records bond `1580264` (`1580264.IB` in later reports), short name
`15贵阳轨道可续期债`, and issuer abbreviation `贵阳市交通运营集团`. The frozen
string added `有限公司` to that abbreviation.

The unique bond code resolves the focal legal issuer under the existing exact-
evidence rule. The current issuer's 2022 bond report defines itself as
`贵阳市公共交通投资运营集团有限公司` and identifies the same bond under the
former name `贵阳市城市轨道交通有限公司`. It also gives current abbreviation
`贵阳市交通运营集团`. A 2023 bond-agent report independently joins the same
bond code and short name to the current legal issuer, Guiyang address, and
unified social credit code `915201006884072115`. A later exchange announcement
explicitly states the former name and repeats that identifier and address.

The evidence also preserves an old-name discrepancy: one bond-agent page uses
`贵阳市城市轨道交通集团有限公司`, while the issuer report and exchange
announcement omit `集团`. The unique bond code, current legal name, unified
social credit code, and address resolve the focal current issuer without
silently harmonizing that discrepancy.

The supported legal issuer is therefore
`贵阳市公共交通投资运营集团有限公司`. The frozen display name remains
unchanged in this experiment; a prospective integration may update only the
supported legal-issuer field.

controlling shareholder and ultimate controller. It also states that the
issuer finances, invests in, constructs, and operates rail-transit projects and
develops related land. A Gui'an government-hosted environment report ties the

infrastructure project.

Under the frozen rules, the evidence supports Guizhou, Guiyang;
`subprovincial_public`; and `eligible`. No new identity, geography, ownership,
or platform-role rule is needed.
