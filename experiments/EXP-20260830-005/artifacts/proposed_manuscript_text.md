# Proposed diagnostic text

This text is proposed for coordinator review only. It should not be inserted
until the full-control encoding is corrected and independently reproduced.

> The sparse-outcome and sample-selection diagnostics identify three limits to
> the adjusted historical-capacity analysis. First, institutional change
> occurs in 12 of the 84 matched gold cases, including 2 substantive exits and
> 10 functional transfers. The complete-control subset contains 78 cases but
> retains all 12 institutional-change outcomes; the 6 excluded cases are all
> nominal exits and have lower historical elite density. Second, the simple
> historical-only association is similar under the LPM, conventional logit,
> and Firth logit, and its LPM sign is unchanged in leave-one-out analyses.
> Third, the current full-control design includes an intercept and two
> exhaustive platform-category indicators, which makes the design matrix rank
> deficient. After one platform category is treated as the reference, the
> adjusted model will still have only 12 events for eight independent columns.
> We therefore treat the 84-row historical-only model as a descriptive
> associational baseline and the adjusted models as sensitivity diagnostics.
> This reporting choice follows the declared estimand and observed-input
> coverage rather than coefficient sign or statistical significance.

The first sentence preserves the frozen binary outcome and reports its two
constituent categories without combining their substantive interpretations.
