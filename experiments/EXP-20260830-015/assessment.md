## Probability-frame candidate

The candidate contains 54 unique eligible legal issuers and retains all 157
originating disclosure rows in a separate origin map. The 54 eligible issuers
account for 58 originating disclosure rows. The frozen proposal has 19 strata:
50 issuer units are in the nominal screen-positive layer and four are in the
screen-nonpositive layer. Every eligible unit has a positive proposed inclusion
probability. The deterministic seed is `20260830015`, but
`random_draw_executed` is `false` throughout.

The strata use only screen status, source-coverage bin, historical-capacity
bin, debt-pressure availability, and administrative level. The builder reads
source-coverage values from the surrogate input but does not read its outcome
columns. No working-reference outcome was accessed, assigned, changed, or
exposed, and no source or screening layer is described as independently human
validated.

## Integrity decision

The result is quarantined because four scope cases and one associated geography
gate remain open, not because the completed decisions or construction tests
failed. The 54-unit file is a reproducible candidate subset, not an approved
or frozen probability frame. PI approval and documented resolution or exclusion
of the four open units are required before a sampling design may be frozen.
