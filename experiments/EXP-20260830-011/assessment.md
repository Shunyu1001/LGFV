# Experiment assessment

## Status

`quarantine`

The experiment preserves a reproducible low-coverage result. The fixed
original-packet hierarchy retrieved and extracted 253 documents for 127 of the
128 unresolved issuer units, but the issuer-specific audit supported a unique
province-city pair for only 40 units. The 31.25 percent combined coverage is
below the predeclared 100-unit threshold. The proposed validation frame remains
unsuitable for a probability draw.

## Source coverage

The 128-unit crosswalk contains 127 source-supported legal identities, 40
unique province-city pairs, 61 supported controlling-owner levels, and 46
issuer-specific excerpts supporting a platform-like public project or
financing role. Only five units contain all four elements. One unit,
`mv_3134f5ad5182` (吉安市井冈山开发区金庐陵经济发展有限公司), has no
inventoried evidence document in the frozen frame. The other 127 units have at
least one retrieved public disclosure; 126 have two fixed-hierarchy records
and one has one record.

The initial extraction produced 87 geography candidates. The entity-linkage
audit reduced this count first to 61 and then to 40 by rejecting subsidiary,
intermediary, predecessor, and generic regulatory references. It also rejected
31 automated conflict candidates across 27 units because the apparent
differences came from third-party locations rather than conflicting issuer
records. No conflict remains among the accepted issuer-specific evidence. The
empty final conflict log records that result without deleting the rejected
candidate counts from the metrics and execution record.

## Scope dispositions

The crosswalk records 13 `provisionally_eligible` issuers, 17
`provisionally_ineligible` issuers, and 98 `review_required` issuers. Among the
98 review-required cases, 67 lack a supported controlling-owner level, 27 have
subprovincial public control but insufficient fixed-source evidence of a
platform-like financing role, and four have municipality-level public control
with the same role-evidence limitation. These groups are exact and mutually
exclusive. The 98 unit identifiers, issuer names, evidence, and unresolved
reasons are retained row by row in
`data/validation/source_supported_validation_geography_scope_crosswalk.csv`.

The scope dispositions are proposed research-audit fields, not final
eligibility decisions. In particular, a local public owner does not establish
city-platform status for a specialized operator, and a provincial or central
owner does not justify assigning a city-platform role that the source does not
document.

## Adverse and null findings

- Eighty-eight of 128 units still lack a unique source-supported province-city
  pair.
- Sixty-seven controlling-owner levels remain unresolved.
- Eighty-two units lack a retained issuer-specific platform-role excerpt.
- Ninety-eight units require human scope review.
- The predeclared 100-unit coverage hypothesis is not supported.
- The accepted evidence does not justify a validation draw, a random seed, a
  national coverage claim, or a classifier-performance estimate.

## Integrity decision

The result is quarantined rather than repaired from names. No exit label,
model prediction, screen status, confidence score, rationale, random seed, or
selected-case field appears in the crosswalk. All retrieved PDFs remained
temporary and uncommitted. The retrieval manifest records publisher, page and
download URLs, title, date, retrieval date, access status, rights note, file
hash, and extraction status for every attempted document.

## Review gates

The PI must approve or revise all 128 scope dispositions, the inclusion of the
five preexisting geography-complete units, the 133-issuer target frame, and the
proposed sampling allocation before any sample is drawn. The 98
review-required cases need additional authoritative evidence or a documented
boundary decision. The 13 provisionally eligible and 17 provisionally
ineligible cases also require PI confirmation; the provisional fields do not
exercise the review authority reserved in the charter.
