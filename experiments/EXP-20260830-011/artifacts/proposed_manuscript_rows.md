# Proposed validation reporting

The proposed issuer validation frame is not yet ready for sampling. Among the
128 issuers whose geography was incomplete, a fixed search of inventoried
original packet records retrieved 253 public disclosures for 127 issuers. A
strict issuer-specific evidence audit supported a unique province-city pair
for 40 issuers, while 88 remained unresolved. The same audit classified 13
issuers as provisionally eligible for the city-platform frame, 17 as
provisionally ineligible, and 98 as requiring further scope review. These
dispositions require PI approval and do not constitute a completed validation
sample or evidence about classifier performance.
