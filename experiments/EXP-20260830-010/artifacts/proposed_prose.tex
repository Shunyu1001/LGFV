\subsection{Measurement contribution}

Prior research already distinguishes formal policy adoption from changes in
organizational practice and from the achievement of policy objectives
\citep{bromley_powell_2012_decoupling,de_bree_stoopendaal_2020_recoupling}.
Research on local government financing platforms likewise shows that
monitoring-list exit, issuer declarations, debt conversion, and market-oriented
restructuring need not locate where the government-financing or project
function went
\citep{jin_rial_2016_lgfv_ppp,feng_wu_zhang_2022_jiaxing,fang_lu_2024_classified_regulation,li_he_ai_2025_transformation}.
We adapt these concerns to an auditable case-level measure. For each issuer, we
verify a formal event, trace the post-event location of the financing and
project function, and assign one of four mutually exclusive institutional
fates. The contribution is this LGFV-specific evidence design and retained
source packet, not the formal-substantive distinction by itself.

\subsection{Candidate contemporary processes}

The historical analysis is exploratory and associational. Historical elite
density is associated with movement away from nominal exit in simple
specifications, but the proxy is also known to predict modern education,
cultural transmission, and social capital
\citep{chen_kung_ma_2020_keju}. The case evidence is consistent with three
possible contemporary processes. Fiscal absorption occurs when liabilities or
project costs move into approved budgets, statutory debt registers, or
government bonds while quasi-fiscal rollover declines. Bureaucratic
coordination occurs when finance, development-and-reform, state-asset, and
issuer records assign and complete the same reorganization. Project or
state-asset governance occurs when project-level ownership, valuation,
contracts, liabilities, operating rights, and revenue control change. The
present design does not identify these processes as mediators, establish their
historical persistence, or separate them fully from contemporary fiscal
resources and document availability.
