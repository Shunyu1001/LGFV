# Mechanism diagnostics

## Decision

Treat the three mechanisms as candidate contemporary processes. Do not use
them as identified mediators of historical elite density.

| Process | Necessary positive evidence | Principal negative or falsifying evidence | Difference from contemporary fiscal capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiscal absorption | A former LGFV liability or project cost enters an approved public budget, appropriation, statutory debt register, or government-bond allocation, with a corresponding fall in quasi-fiscal rollover. | The obligation moves to another LGFV, subsidiary, trust, wealth-management product, PPP fund, or successor issuer, or is refinanced without a named public assumption. | It records the payer and legal financing instrument, not the size of the local resource envelope or fiscal self-sufficiency. |
| Bureaucratic coordination | Dated and mutually consistent finance-bureau, development-and-reform, SASAC, and issuer records assign responsibilities and show completed staff, data, budget, liability, or project transfers, followed by monitoring and correction. | Agencies repeat a common statement but assignments remain incomplete, hidden continuation appears, implementation reverses, or coordination chiefly sustains concealment or an implicit guarantee. | It records implementation across agencies among places with similar resources; official contact, political ties, ratings, and low yields do not suffice. |
| Project or state-asset governance | Project-level title, valuation, registration, concession, contract, liability, operating-right, personnel, and revenue records show who controls each transferred project or asset after the event. | A debt swap occurs without a functional transfer, or regrouping preserves entrusted construction, land-conveyance dependence, fiscal subsidies, government-appointed control, or public repayment expectations. | It records legal and operating control over assets and contracts, which can remain weak despite ample fiscal resources or completed debt conversion. |

## Coding separation

The same sentence should not count as evidence for all three processes. A
subsidy or receivable can identify a payer for fiscal absorption, but it does
not establish cross-agency implementation or legal control over an asset.
Bureaucratic coordination requires a decision chain and completion evidence.
Asset governance requires project-level ownership, contractual responsibility,
and operating control.

Each process code needs a dated positive rule, a dated negative rule, a source
quality field, and a missing-evidence state. Absence of a public document is
not negative evidence unless the relevant disclosure is legally required and
the search packet records where it should have appeared.

## Competing explanations

- Financing substitution can mimic absorption when a bank loan is replaced by
  a municipal corporate bond, trust claim, PPP structure, or successor issuer.
- Coordinated evasion can mimic bureaucratic capacity when multiple agencies
  use consistent formal language while the function continues.
- Political connections and bailout expectations can mimic coordination by
  lowering yields without completing a reorganization.
- Modern education, educational infrastructure, social capital, province, and
  document availability can generate an association between historical elite
  density and the observed fate without persistent administrative capacity.
- Delayed recoupling can make an early post-event snapshot look like persistent
  nominal exit. Repeated observations are necessary.

## Minimum stronger design

A stronger mechanism claim would require independently coded process measures,
temporal ordering from formal event to reassignment to operational outcome,
negative cases with comparable contemporary fiscal resources, and tests for
document-availability and province-level confounding. The sparse historical
sample does not support conventional mediation analysis. A within-case process
claim should be made only when the required positive evidence appears and the
principal falsifier does not.
