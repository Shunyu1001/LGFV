# Proposed independent-coding reporting

The independent-coding stage has been prepared but not executed. A blinded
instrument covers all 94 frozen gold cases and provides case identity,
geography, document identifiers, and source locations without exposing any
current label, confidence, rationale, model output, or reviewer field. A
separate adjudication template remains blank. These materials do not identify
inter-coder agreement because no second human has yet completed the packet.

The candidate validation instrument is more limited. Only four provisionally
eligible issuers also have source-supported geography and packet-ready source
locations; nine additional provisionally eligible scope cases remain omitted
because their geography is unresolved. No probability sample has been drawn,
and the candidate packet does not represent an approved validation frame.

These paragraphs are proposed artifacts only. The central manuscript remains
unchanged.
