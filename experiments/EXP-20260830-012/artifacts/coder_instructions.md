# Independent coding instructions

## Purpose

These packets support genuine independent human coding under the frozen LGFV
exit-type codebook. They do not contain a second set of labels. The second
human coder must record every coding field without consulting the current gold
labels, LLM outputs, reviewer notes, or the proposed validation strata.

## Packet use

The gold packet contains all 94 frozen gold case identifiers and source
locations. The candidate packet contains four geography-verified,
provisionally eligible issuer units from the geography and scope audit. Nine
additional provisionally eligible scope cases are omitted because their
geography remains unresolved. The candidate packet is not a sample
and must not be circulated until the PI approves the target frame and scope
decisions. Before either packet is circulated, the source steward should
confirm access to the rights-approved source archive or the recorded public
disclosure URLs.

For each case, the coder should first determine whether the unit meets the
codebook's inclusion rules. The coder should then identify the formal event,
the pre-event platform function, and the post-event location of the financing
or quasi-fiscal function. The coder may select substantive exit, nominal exit,
functional transfer, liquidation, or mark the evidence as unclear. Confidence,
source coverage, continued-function evidence, the most plausible alternative,
source references, ambiguity, and the remaining caveat should be recorded in
the blank fields. The coder should sign and freeze the completed packet before
any comparison with the original coding.

## Adjudication

The adjudication template is separate from the coder-facing packet. It should
be opened only after the second coder's entries are frozen. The original coder
and second coder should then record their labels, identify whether any
disagreement concerns the formal event, post-event function, related-entity
scope, source hierarchy, or an edge-case rule, and document the adjudicated
label and source basis. No disagreement should be overwritten or removed.

## Review gates

Creating these blank files does not establish inter-coder agreement and does
not close reviewer issue R-003. Raw agreement, category-specific agreement,
Cohen's kappa or an appropriate sparse-category alternative, and adjudication
counts can be computed only after a real second human independently completes
the gold packet. The PI must separately approve the candidate frame and
probability allocation before any sample is drawn.
