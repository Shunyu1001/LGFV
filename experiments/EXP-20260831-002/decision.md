# Multiple-location decisions

## Shenzhen International

exchange-filed interim report each distinguish Bermuda registration, a Hong
Kong head office and principal place of business, and a Shenzhen office. The
prospectus also identifies Shenzhen city control. These are current facts about
the same focal legal issuer, not discrepancies between weaker and stronger
sources. The frozen source hierarchy therefore cannot choose among them.

Any unique mainland assignment requires a validation-design rule.
`CR-20260831-001` records a prospective proposal, alternatives, consequences,
and acceptance tests. The proposal is not implemented. The Experiment 001
geography gate remains open.

## Dongyangguang

The evidence resolves the geography bottleneck to Guangdong, Shenzhen under
the existing focal-entity rule. The 2026 prospectus labels Shenzhen as the
issuer's legal domicile and separately labels Dongguan as the information-
disclosure officer's contact address. The institutions and reference-file
sections also give the issuer address as Shenzhen. A rating report and a legal
opinion corroborate the legal entity, unified social credit code, Shenzhen
registration authority, and Shenzhen domicile.

This is not a choice between two valid issuer locations. A disclosure officer's
contact address is outside the focal issuer geography unless a source also
labels it as an issuer office. The Dongguan evidence is retained in the excerpt
register, and its Dongguan telephone and fax details are not discarded.

The existing registered frame has not been rebuilt. The Experiment 001 gate
therefore remains open in the tracked crosswalk until the separately registered
`EXP-20260831-004` integration is reviewed and executed.
