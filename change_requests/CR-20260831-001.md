# Change request: Issuer location concepts and unique mainland assignment

## Status

- Change request: `CR-20260831-001`
- Opened: `2026-08-31T12:40:00+08:00`
- Object: validation geography design
- Status: proposed; PI decision required
- Evidence experiment: `EXP-20260831-002`
- Implemented: no

## Observed design gap

The frozen source grammar permits an issuer's registered address, domicile,
office location, registration authority, controlling owner, or named
supervising government as geography evidence. It does not define how those
concepts determine one mainland province-city when a focal issuer validly has
more than one location.

`mv_2547f5fbc2e2`, Shenzhen International Holdings Limited, exposes the gap.
The issuer is incorporated and registered in Bermuda, reports its head office
and principal place of business in Hong Kong, and reports a Shenzhen office.
Its controlling shareholder and ultimate controller are tied to Shenzhen. The
source hierarchy does not resolve this conflict because the same authoritative
issuer records report all locations. A unique mainland city therefore requires
a substantive rule, not a reliability judgment.

The Dongyangguang audit does not require this change. Its Dongguan field is
explicitly the information-disclosure officer's contact address, while the
issuer's legal domicile, registration authority, unified social credit code,
and issuer address point to Shenzhen. Excluding a third-party contact field
from issuer geography applies the existing focal-entity rule rather than a new
address precedence.

## Proposed prospective rule

Record, but do not collapse, the following concepts for every audited issuer:

1. legal jurisdiction of incorporation;
2. legal domicile or registered address;
3. head office or principal place of business;
4. issuer office locations;
5. controlling-owner or supervising-government nexus; and
6. disclosure-officer, intermediary, or other third-party contact addresses.

Define `analysis_city` prospectively as follows:

1. Use the focal legal issuer's mainland legal domicile or registered address
   when it identifies one province-city.
2. If the focal legal issuer has no mainland legal domicile, use a mainland
   city only when an authoritative source identifies an issuer head office or
   office there and authoritative ownership evidence identifies the same city
   government or city SASAC as ultimate controller. Record the offshore legal
   jurisdiction and any non-mainland principal place separately.
3. If two locations at the applicable step identify different mainland cities,
   or the ownership and issuer-office nexus do not identify the same city,
   retain `source_supported_multiple` or `unresolved_after_search`.
4. Never treat a disclosure officer's, underwriter's, lawyer's, accountant's,
   rating agency's, or other intermediary's contact address as an issuer
   location unless the document separately labels it as an issuer office.

Under this proposal, Shenzhen International would receive `analysis_city =
Shenzhen`, with Bermuda legal jurisdiction and Hong Kong principal place
retained as conflicting location concepts. The rule would not change the
issuer's existing `ineligible` scope disposition.

## Alternatives

1. **Strict legal-domicile rule.** Require a mainland legal domicile and
   exclude or leave unresolved any offshore-domiciled issuer. This is simpler
   but discards explicit local-controller and mainland-office evidence.
2. **Principal-place rule.** Prefer head office or principal place of business.
   This would assign Shenzhen International outside mainland China and would
   require a policy for Hong Kong observations.
3. **No unique city for ineligible units.** Permit an already ineligible unit
   to retain multiple geography. This relaxes the current full-frame geography
   integrity gate and changes validation coverage reporting.

## Falsifiable acceptance tests

Before approval or implementation:

1. Apply the candidate rule blindly to all 133 units and publish a field-level
   audit showing every legal jurisdiction, domicile, principal place, issuer
   office, controller nexus, and third-party contact used.
2. Require zero assignments derived only from name parsing, search snippets,
   disclosure-officer addresses, or intermediary addresses.
3. Require deterministic repeatability and a case-diff listing every unit whose
   province, city, geography status, or scope disposition changes relative to
   Experiment 001.
4. Require the Shenzhen International test fixture to retain Bermuda, Hong
   Kong, and Shenzhen simultaneously while assigning a unique analysis city
   only if step 2 of the rule is met.
5. Require the Dongyangguang fixture to resolve to Shenzhen without invoking
   the offshore fallback and to preserve the Dongguan contact as contradictory
   operational evidence outside issuer geography.
6. Require same-priority mainland conflicts to remain nonunique.
7. Require all immutable, label, origin-row, and no-random-draw checks to pass.
8. Report the resulting full-frame counts before the PI authorizes a freeze.

## Consequences and approval boundary

This proposal changes the validation design and cannot be adopted through a
case memo or source hierarchy interpretation. Until the PI approves one rule
and a separately registered experiment passes the tests above, the Experiment
001 frame remains quarantined and Shenzhen International retains an open
geography gate. Approval does not authorize a sample draw, manuscript change,
or representation of this Codex audit as independent human validation.

## Exact PI decision requested

Choose one of the proposed rule, the strict legal-domicile alternative, the
principal-place alternative, or the explicit relaxation for ineligible units;
or direct that Shenzhen International remain nonunique and be excluded from an
approved frame. Record the selected policy, effective date, and authorized
prospective test. No default choice is assumed.
