# Research Charter

## Research question

How can researchers distinguish formal LGFV exit from functional fiscal
change, and what local conditions are associated with the institutional fate
of officially exiting platforms?

## Primary contribution

The primary contribution is a measurement framework that follows the
post-event location of the government financing function. The framework
distinguishes substantive exit, nominal exit, functional transfer, and
liquidation. Its purpose is to measure whether formal compliance corresponds
to functional institutional change.

## Secondary application

The paper uses historically rooted state capacity to explain variation in exit
fate. The current evidence supports an exploratory associational application,
not a causal estimate. The historical-capacity claim must remain secondary to
the measurement contribution unless a later approved design resolves the
present sample, outcome-imbalance, control-quality, and identification limits.

## Unit and outcomes

The unit is a city-platform case tied to a documented formal event and a
post-event source packet. The four outcomes are defined in
`coding/codebook.md`. The current gold-standard snapshot contains 94
human-validated cases: 2 substantive exits, 82 nominal exits, 10 functional
transfers, and no liquidation cases. The current historical-capacity analysis
uses 84 matched gold cases.

## Current analysis labels

- Measurement validation: exploratory development with human-reviewed labels.
- Descriptive outcome distribution: descriptive within the assembled sample.
- Historical-capacity models: exploratory and associational.
- Contemporary-control models: first-pass diagnostics.
- LLM surrogate adjustment: one-sided nominal-exit screening validation, not a
  validated four-class classifier.

## Integrity commitments

The project reports null, negative, contradictory, and unstable estimates.
Specification choice cannot depend on coefficient sign or significance. LLM
labels remain auxiliary measurements. Material claims require traceable source
or experiment identifiers. The human PI retains responsibility for data use,
interpretation, and submission.

## Review-gated changes

Human approval is required to alter the four-category outcome scale, gold-label
eligibility, the primary measurement contribution, the validation design, the
principal historical-capacity measure, the main estimand, or the strength of
causal and novelty claims.
