# Analysis Plan

## Current objectives

The paper has three ordered empirical objectives. First, it evaluates whether
the four exit types can be coded transparently from documentary evidence.
Second, it describes the distribution and source-supported content of those
labels in the assembled sample. Third, it uses the labels to examine an
exploratory association between historical state capacity and movement away
from nominal exit.

## Measurement analysis

The gold outcome is the human-reviewed final label. Source coverage, continued
function evidence, confidence, an alternative label, and the final rationale
must accompany each case. The next validation stage must report independent
double-coding on a defined subset, raw agreement, category-specific agreement,
Cohen's kappa or an appropriate alternative, and adjudication counts.

The LLM surrogate is currently a one-sided screen for nominal exit. Its present
overlap check estimates positive predictive performance only. Recall,
false-negative behavior, and multiclass performance are not identified by the
current overlap sample and must not be implied.

## Descriptive analysis

Report sample construction, attrition, outcome counts, source-quality
distribution, and representative source packets. Population shares are not
identified by the current assembled sample. Any augmented sample using
surrogate labels must keep disclosure-level and issuer-level units separate.

## Exploratory explanatory analysis

The declared binary exploratory outcome equals one for substantive exit or
functional transfer and zero for nominal exit. This outcome asks whether a
case moves beyond narrow nominal compliance; it does not equate substantive
exit with functional transfer. Liquidation remains a separate category and is
currently absent from the gold sample.

The baseline exposure is standardized Ming-Qing elite density matched to the
contemporary prefecture. The baseline simple specifications use elite density,
a high-capacity indicator, and an ordered capacity-bin rank. Contemporary
controls include GDP per capita, fiscal self-sufficiency, statutory debt
pressure, land-finance dependence, source or disclosure quality, platform
administrative level, capital or sub-provincial status, and province fixed
effects when estimable.

Because institutional change occurs in only 12 of 84 matched gold cases and
substantive exit occurs twice, the current LPMs are descriptive diagnostics.
Multinomial models and adjusted historical-capacity claims require a larger and
less imbalanced outcome sample or a design justified for sparse outcomes.

## Missing data and model reporting

Do not silently impute missing contemporary controls. Report model-specific
sample sizes and compare included and excluded observations. Any proposed
imputation, alternative clustering, weighting, fixed-effect change, or sample
restriction requires a pre-result experiment brief and, when it changes the
main estimand, a change request.

## Required reporting

Every model table must identify the outcome, unit, sample, variable coding,
standard-error method, fixed effects, coefficient scale, and sample size.
Narrative effect sizes must be generated from or checked against the underlying
table. Exploratory and validation-adjusted estimates must be labeled as such.
